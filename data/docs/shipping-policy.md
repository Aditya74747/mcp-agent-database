# Shipping Policy

_Owner: Logistics · Last reviewed: 2025-08-12_

## Delivery times
| Region | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| EU (Germany, France, Netherlands, Spain, Poland, Sweden) | 2-4 business days | next business day |
| United States | 5-8 business days | 2 business days |
| India | 6-10 business days | 3-4 business days |

## Costs
- Standard shipping is **free for orders over EUR 150**; below that it costs EUR 6.90.
- Express shipping costs **EUR 19** in the EU and EUR 39 elsewhere.

## Order status lifecycle
`shipped` means the parcel has left the warehouse; `delivered` is set when the carrier
confirms delivery. Orders still `shipped` after 14 days are escalated to the carrier.
